Windows 98 Development Journal

Entry #1: The Beginning

January 15, 1998

Today marks the beginning of an exciting journey. We're developing Max the Dog to be the ultimate companion for Windows 98 users. The goal is to create an interactive assistant that makes navigating the internet easier and more enjoyable.

Our team is inspired by Microsoft's Clippy but aims to make something even better - a digital friend that truly understands and helps users in their daily computing tasks.

Entry #2: First Prototype

March 22, 1998

We've completed our first working prototype! Max can now perform basic navigation tasks and respond to simple commands. The animation system is coming along nicely, and we're working on integrating web browsing capabilities.

The team is excited about the progress. We're on track for a beta release by the end of the year.

Entry #3: Beta Testing

October 5, 1998

Beta testing has begun! We've received great feedback from our initial testers. Users love Max's personality and find him helpful for organizing bookmarks and managing downloads.

Some users have requested additional features like game generation and price comparison tools. We're considering these for future updates.

Entry #4: Official Release

December 20, 1998

Max the Dog is now officially released! We're proud to offer this free software to the Windows 98 community. The response has been overwhelmingly positive.

We're already planning updates and new features. The Supporter Plan and Premium Package options will help us continue development and provide even better features in the future.

Entry #5: Looking Forward

January 10, 1999

As we enter 1999, we're excited about the future of Max the Dog. We're working on compatibility with Windows NT and planning for Windows 2000 support.

Thank you to all our users for their support and feedback. Max wouldn't be what he is today without you!
